Deep in the Himalayas lays an India minimally affected by British colonialism, where centuries old traditions are preserved, where wandering priests and monks strive to reach the pinnacle of minalism in their quest to attain Moksha, or Enlightenment. This attire is nothing but a simple piece of cloth, for its purpose is to simply shield one's bare skin from the extremities of nature; tempestuous winds, volatile rain clouds, and snow squalls are quite expected. In a time where ancient traditions are met with a new challenge, keeping true to their roots in midst of touristic generation, living this lifestyle is in and of itself a form of deep meditation, proving that if the mind can be disciplined, anything is possible.
