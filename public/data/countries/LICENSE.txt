Detailed country maps derived from Natural Earth 1:10m geographic data (public domain).
Datasets: ne_10m_admin_0_countries, ne_10m_admin_1_states_provinces, ne_10m_populated_places_simple.
Source: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/tree/master/geojson
Terms: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
Only journal destination countries are included. Geometry is simplified at 0.0015 degrees (0.0006 for Puerto Rico), then coordinates are rounded to four decimal places. Main-map extents and named geographic insets are presentation choices. Country and regional boundaries follow the source dataset.
